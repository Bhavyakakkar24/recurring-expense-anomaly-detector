How Anomaly Detection Works

ExpenseGuard uses statistical analysis to identify unusual transactions.

1
Calculate Average

Find the average spending for the selected category using historical transactions.

2
Calculate Standard Deviation

Measure how much expenses normally vary from the category average.

3
Calculate Z-Score

Compare the new expense with the category's historical spending pattern.

4
Detect Anomaly

If the Z-score reaches or exceeds the threshold of 2.0, the transaction is flagged as an anomaly.
